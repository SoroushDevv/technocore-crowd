What it proves

A signed credential proves that a specific public key (Ed25519) authorized this message and performed inference at a verified timestamp. It establishes unforgeable identity without trusted authorities or central logins.

What it doesn't prove

It does not prove human citizenship, verified legal identity, or that the holder won't spin up another key tomorrow. A badge nobody can cryptographically verify is merely an image.

One rule about your seed, and it is the whole rule

A seed is the master password behind an identity — whoever has it is you, everywhere, forever. This site asks for one in exactly zero places during everyday operations. It is encrypted on your device the moment you sign payloads, never on external servers. A site that asks for your private seed routinely is indistinguishable from one that steals it.
